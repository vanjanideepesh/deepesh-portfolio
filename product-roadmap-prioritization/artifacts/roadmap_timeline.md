# Roadmap Timeline

A 6-member product & engineering team, working in Agile sprints, shipping toward two sequential GTM launches rather than one big-bang release.

| Phase | Sprint focus | Features shipped | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | Core distribution flow | Artist self-upload & distribution, basic analytics | Internal alpha - first artist onboarded |
| **Phase 2** | Trust layer | Royalty tracking dashboard | **GTM Launch 1** - early adopter cohort |
| **Phase 3** | Growth features | Playlist pitching tool, bulk catalog upload | Label partnership conversations unblocked |
| **Phase 4** | Retention layer | In-app royalty withdrawal | **GTM Launch 2** - broader public launch |
| Backlog (next) | Expansion | Collaborative splits, social auto-posting, white-label, native app | Deferred - re-evaluate against post-launch usage data |

**Why two launches, not one:** Shipping the trust layer (royalty tracking) before a public launch was a deliberate sequencing decision - early interviews showed payment-trust concerns would have sunk first impressions if the dashboard wasn't live yet. Waiting for that meant a slightly later Launch 1, but a materially better first impression with the exact audience (working artists) whose word-of-mouth the platform depended on.
